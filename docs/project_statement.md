# Reactive Multi-View Driving World Model

Goal:
Build a small-scale research prototype for action-conditioned single-view-to-multiview driving video generation.

Research alignment:
Reactive world models, closed-loop generation, temporal consistency, multiview coherence, autonomous-driving simulation.

Core question:
Can a small model generate short-horizon multiview driving video conditioned on a front-camera context and ego actions, while preserving temporal and geometric consistency better than simple baselines?

Non-goals:
No SOTA photorealism.
No massive model.
No full autonomous-driving stack.
No proprietary industrial data.
